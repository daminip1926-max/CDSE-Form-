FAQ – CDSE, DHARMA & EAP

Q1. Where should the date of the CDSE/inspection visit be uploaded in DHARMA? +
A: The date of the CDSE/inspection visit should be entered in the relevant CDSE/Inspection module of DHARMA, along with the required inspection details.
Q2. What should be done after the Comprehensive Dam Safety Evaluation report is signed? +
A: After signing by the concerned experts/Panel of Experts, the signed CDSE report should be submitted to the Dam Owner through the prescribed DHARMA process.
Q3. What should the Dam Owner do after receiving the CDSE report? +
A: The Dam Owner should examine the observations/recommendations and submit the required Interim Report/Action Taken Report and Final Report, as applicable, within the prescribed timeline.
Q4. Where should the Dam Owner submit the Interim and Final Reports? +
A: The Dam Owner should upload/submit the Interim and Final Reports in the designated DHARMA module, along with the required compliance/action taken against the CDSE recommendations.
Q5. Is the EAP for Sunny Day Condition available in DHARMA? +
A: The availability of the Sunny Day Condition EAP should be verified in the respective dam's EAP section/documents in DHARMA. If unavailable, the Dam Owner should be advised to prepare/update the EAP and upload the approved document in DHARMA.
Q6. What CDSE-related information should be updated in DHARMA? +
A: Important milestones such as date of visit, inspection status, signed CDSE report, submission to Dam Owner, Interim Report, Final Report, compliance/action taken and pending actions should be updated regularly for effective monitoring and traceability.
Q7. What action should be taken for delayed submission by the Dam Owner? +
A: The concerned SDSO should monitor pending submissions through DHARMA and issue reminders for delays. Persistent non-compliance should be escalated to the competent authority for appropriate action.
